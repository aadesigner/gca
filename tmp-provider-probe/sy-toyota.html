Comprehensive information about Toyota

Toyota was founded by Kiichiro Toyoda in 1937, with its headquarters located in the city of Toyota, Aichi Prefecture, Japan. Toyota is recognized as one of the largest and leading companies in the global automotive industry and enjoys a strong reputation in the Saudi market due to the reliability and high efficiency of its vehicles. The company is distinguished by its wide range of models that meet the diverse needs of customers, from sedans such as the Toyota Camry to versatile SUVs like the Toyota Fortuner and Toyota Land Cruiser, as well as the renowned pickup trucks such as the Toyota Hilux. This extensive lineup makes Toyota the preferred choice for many individuals and families in the Kingdom.
